Our Story

Students were paying a fortune to be kept in the dark

Traditional agencies in Dhaka file applications behind locked agent portals, keeping portal passwords secret and pushing whichever institute pays them the highest back-end commission.

StudySail was born to flip that paradigm. We operate completely online via Google Meet and Zoom, mentoring students from Dhaka, Chittagong, Sylhet, Rajshahi, and abroad. We evaluate your profile, shortlist public universities honestly, and coach you live on screen as you submit your own applications.

The result: you master the skills to write your SOPs, email international research supervisors, and confidently defend your case at any embassy visa interview.
